    Tone curve

    Re-score photos

    Scoring measures sharpness and exposure and detects faces. Pick what this project is a shoot of and it detects that too: photos without one sort down, sharpness is measured on the subject instead of the whole frame, and look-alike subjects get grouped.

    Decisions and edits are preserved. Grouping runs straight afterwards, because scoring rebuilds the face embeddings the groups are built from.

    Subject groups

    Grouping is by appearance — colour and how it sits on the shape. Two cars of the same colour and shape will land in one group; rename or hide anything it gets wrong. Changing what is detected is part of ↻ rescore.

    Apply adjustments to many

    Apply an edit to several photos at once. Non-destructive — the originals are never modified.

    Photos not found

    This project's photo folder isn't where it used to be (moved, renamed, or on a drive that's disconnected). Pick the new location and I'll re-link the photos — matching by folder structure first, then by file name for anything that moved around.
