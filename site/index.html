Cull and edit a shoot on your own machine.

Pickapicka scores every frame for sharpness and exposure, groups the shoot into scenes and suggests what to keep. Then it edits the keepers, non-destructively. Your photos never leave the computer they are on.

Scores every frame

Sharpness and exposure for each photo, weighed against the rest of its scene, and a pick, review or reject suggestion to start from.

One key per photo

P pick, R reject, V review, and on to the next. Stars and colour labels as in Lightroom, kept in every export.

Nothing is uploaded

It runs on your machine and leaves your photos where they are, untouched. Every decision lives in the project's own folder.

Culling

The first pass, done for you.

Point it at a folder of a few hundred or a few thousand frames. It groups them into scenes, by folder or by the pauses in the capture times, and suggests the keepers in each. You decide, one key at a time.

  • Subject detection sorts down the frames that missed the car, and measures sharpness on the subject rather than on the whole frame.
  • Faces grouped into people; focus peaking that measures edge steepness, not contrast; a loupe at real pixels.
  • Compare two to four frames of a burst side by side, zoomed and panned together.
  • HDR brackets found from EXIF and merged into one frame to cull.

Editing

An editor for the keepers.

Everything is non-destructive and previewed at the resolution you export at, down to the original pixels at 100%. RAW files are first-class.

  • The full slider set and a tone curve, colour mixer and grading.
  • Up to 16 local masks: radial, gradient, brush and tonal range, or selected for you: subject, background, skin, face, hair, clothes.
  • Crop and straighten, lens and perspective correction, film emulation built as a chain rather than a filter.
  • Watermarks from EXIF templates, and presets that stack.
