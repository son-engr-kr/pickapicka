Pickapicka generative fill model (sd15-inpaint-lcm)

This is a modified version of two models, each under its own licence, both
included here:

  Stable Diffusion v1.5 inpainting, Runway ML, as mirrored at
  https://huggingface.co/stable-diffusion-v1-5/stable-diffusion-inpainting

  Licence: CreativeML Open RAIL++-M (LICENSE-CreativeML-OpenRAIL++-M.txt)

What was changed, by the Pickapicka project:

  - The LCM-LoRA was fused into the inpainting UNet's weights (scale 1.0).
  - The UNet, the VAE encoder (returning the latent distribution's mean) and
    the VAE decoder were exported to ONNX (opset 17): unet.onnx,
    vae_encoder.onnx, vae_decoder.onnx and their .onnx.data weight files.
  - The UNet's attention was exported through diffusers' classic attention
    processor and fused by onnxruntime's transformer optimizer into
    Attention and MultiHeadAttention nodes.
  - Their float weights are stored as float16, each followed by a cast back
    to float32 in the graph.
  - The tokenizer and the text encoder are not included. empty_prompt.npy is
    the text encoder's output for the empty prompt, computed once.
